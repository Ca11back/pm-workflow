# Prototype readiness

Use this bounded check after current-scope Decisions and scenarios are resolved, before Definition approval.

## Walk every critical journey once

For each current-scope journey, verify from approved behavior:

1. first entry, actor, permission, and required inputs;
2. initiating action and business guard;
3. immediate visible result and side effects;
4. material failure, cancellation, or recovery;
5. asynchronous result and later re-entry/retrieval when applicable;
6. content or commitment the Experience must render.

Record a concise result in `Prototype readiness walkthrough` and set `Unresolved prototype blockers` to a concrete list or `none` only after the sweep. Do not prescribe a fixed page, tab, or navigation pattern.

## Consolidate gaps

When several independent gaps are visible, finish this bounded read-only sweep before asking. Present two or three independent decisions together when practical; give each one recommendation, strongest trade-off, and permission to answer outside the options. Keep dependent questions sequential. If two consecutive prototype/discovery corrections still expose foundational behavior gaps, stop local patching and review the whole current Definition once.

## Use exploration only when it adds evidence

Optional exploration is justified when a textual walkthrough cannot distinguish materially different visible flows because of multiple roles, asynchronous stages, non-trivial recovery, cross-entry retrieval, or conflicting evidence.

Pass one bounded unresolved Decision/Rule set to the Experience role. Its local Pen output under `draft/exploration/` is provisional evidence only. It cannot be approved, frozen, or used to decide behavior. Bring the observations back to Definition, resolve the decisions, and then create the formal Experience Brief normally.
